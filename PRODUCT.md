# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators reviewing Thanon's skills and project work on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present Thanon's background, skills, and selected projects clearly, with enough detail to understand each project's role and features without making the home page feel crowded.

## Brand Personality

Focused, technical, and confident.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template layouts, crowded controls, vague marketing language, hidden navigation, and decorative effects that reduce readability.

## Design Principles

- Show real project work before decoration.
- Keep navigation obvious and easy to use on every screen size.
- Use short, plain English descriptions.
- Keep the midnight-blue visual identity consistent.
- Separate quick project summaries from detailed project pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep text contrast strong, controls keyboard accessible, touch targets easy to press, media labelled, and motion respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
